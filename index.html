Problem Diagnosis

Five logins before your first coffee.

If your business lives in five places, you are the only thing holding it together.

The MLS in one tab. Your website in another. Email here, texts there, social somewhere else. A follow-up slips through a gap between systems. A closing deadline lives in an email thread you have to dig for. A license renewal sneaks up because the reminder sits in a tool you stopped opening.

None of it is a skill problem. You are good at this work. It is a plumbing problem: every tool does one job, none of them talk to each other, and you are the connector, at night, after the showings are done.

Meridian was built by watching exactly this. One workspace where the listings, the marketing, the deals, and the documents already know about each other. The follow-ups stop slipping. The deadlines surface themselves. And the connector, you, gets to go home.
